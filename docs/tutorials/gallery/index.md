## Advanced

An external, independently-implemented cross-check: [lmfit](https://lmfit.github.io/lmfit-py/)
as an oracle, not just spectrafit-core's own alternate solvers — the same
idea the project's own benchmark harness runs at scale, distilled into one
readable script.

<div class="grid cards" markdown>

-   ![4-peak fit (2 Gaussian + 2 Lorentzian + linear background) compared between spectrafit and lmfit](_static/spectrafit_vs_lmfit_moderate.png){ width="300" }

    **[spectrafit vs. lmfit — a moderately complex spectrum](spectrafit_vs_lmfit_moderate.md)**

    Four overlapping peaks of mixed shape on a sloped background, fitted independently by spectrafit-core and a hand-built lmfit composite — agreeing to within `4e-6`.

</div>

## Extra

The genuinely messy case: more peaks, mixed lineshapes, and a
physically-motivated tied parameter — where "do the two independent
optimizers actually agree" stops being a given.

    An XPS-style, 8-peak, three-lineshape spectrum with a tied spin-orbit doublet linewidth — spectrafit and lmfit converge to matching R² and a loose per-parameter tolerance, honestly reported rather than overclaimed.
